My Favorite Person :

Sam Smith

My love for Sam smith

My journey with Sam Smith began in 2017 when I fell in love with The Thrill of It All, a flawless album where every single track resonated deeply with me. Following his career back to In the Lonely Hour, I realized I had actually been a fan since 2014. Beyond the incredible discography, his pride in his sexuality inspires me. True art transcends boundaries, and my respect for people spans all genders, colors, races, and religions. During challenging moments of low self-esteem, his powerful anthem "Love Me More" provided comfort, healing, and the strength to embrace who I am.

Here are some of my favorite albums :

  • In The Lonely Hour (2014)
  • The Thrill of It All (2017)
  • Love Goes (2020)

Some of my favorite singles from their albums include :

1. Stay With Me

2. Too Good At Goodbyes

3. So Serious
